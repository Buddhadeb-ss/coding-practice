> & Queues → Trees/BST → Searching/Sorting/Hashing → Graphs.

> for later.

# Rule

**Do not try to finish everything.**

For each college topic:

1.  Learn the topic.
2.  Solve the easiest relevant LeetCode problems.
3.  Write the solution yourself in **C**.
4.  Understand the time and space complexity.
5.  Move on.

The harder LeetCode problems are intentionally kept toward the end. The
goal right now is **DSA fluency + C implementation**, not interview
preparation.
