## Rule

-   Prefer **Easy** problems.
-   Solve them in **C**.
-   Do not worry about solving every problem.
-   If a problem is taking too long, learn the concept and move on.
-   PB Hustle remains the separate place for harder, unfamiliar
    problem-solving.
